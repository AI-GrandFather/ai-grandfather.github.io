Data Deletion – Match Tales

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Match Tales is developed by Mian Muhammad Athar (AI Grandfather). The game has no accounts.

Request Deletion by Email

Email atharmushtaq9@gmail.com with the subject “Data Deletion Request – Match Tales” to have analytics data tied to your device deleted. Include your device type and roughly when you installed the game. We reply within 7 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Delete Data on Your Device

Uninstall the game to delete all game data stored on your device: progress, coins, boosters, settings and purchase records kept by the game.

Advertising Data (Google)

  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • iOS: turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track” in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Learn more: Google partner sites policy and Firebase privacy.

What Is Deleted and What May Be Kept

  • Deleted: analytics data linked to your device’s app instance ID, and your support emails.
  • May be kept: aggregated statistics that don’t identify anyone, and records we must keep by law.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google or Apple under their own policies.
