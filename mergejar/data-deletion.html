Data Deletion

Updated November 26, 2026

Merge Jar does not require an account. Scores, jar progress, coins, settings and rewards are stored in the app’s local data.

Delete local game data

  1. On iPhone or iPad, touch and hold the app icon and choose Remove App, then Delete App. Offloading keeps app data.
  2. On Android, open Settings, choose Merge Jar under Apps and clear its storage, or uninstall the app. Menu names vary by device.

This removes local progress from the device. Manage any existing backups separately in your device settings.

Support and advertising records

To request deletion of personal information you sent to support, email atharmushtaq9@gmail.com from the address you originally used. Ad providers handle their own data. See the provider links and choices in the Privacy Policy.
