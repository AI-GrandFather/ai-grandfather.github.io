Match Tales: Puzzle Adventure

Swap, match and blast your way through thousands of colorful puzzle levels!

Match Tales is a bright match 3 puzzle game. Line up three or more pieces of the same color, clear each level’s goals before you run out of moves, and build powerful combos.

  • Make Rockets, TNT, Propellers and the Prism Orb, then swap two together for bigger combos.
  • Clear boxes, grass, cupboards, mailboxes and dozens of other obstacles.
  • Win streaks, daily rewards and helpful boosters.
  • Plays offline. No account needed.

FAQ

Is it free? Yes. Match Tales is free to play, with optional in-app purchases.

Does it work offline? Yes. Every level plays without an internet connection. Ads, purchases and analytics simply wait until you are online.

Are there forced ads? No. The game only shows rewarded videos, and only when you choose to watch one for a reward.
