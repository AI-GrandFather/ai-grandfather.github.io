Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Match Tales (“the game”), developed by Mian Muhammad Athar, publishing as AI Grandfather (“we”, “us”), handles information. Contact: atharmushtaq9@gmail.com.

Summary

  • No accounts, no login and no chat.
  • Your progress, coins and purchases are saved on your device.
  • The game uses Google AdMob (rewarded ads only) and Google Firebase (analytics and game settings).
  • We do not sell your personal data.

Information We Collect

Collected automatically by Google services in the game:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): IP address (used for approximate location), app interactions, diagnostics, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID (on iOS, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking).
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: anonymous gameplay events (for example levels started, won and lost, purchases and ad views), the app instance ID, device model, operating system, language and country or approximate region.
  • Firebase Remote Config: the Firebase installation ID, app version, operating system version, language and country, used to deliver game settings such as difficulty and economy tuning.

Information you send us: if you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message.

How We Use Information

  • To show optional rewarded ads and measure them.
  • To understand how the game is played and improve levels, difficulty and stability.
  • To deliver game settings and prevent fraud and cheating.
  • To answer your support requests.

Advertising and Consent

The game only shows rewarded video ads, and only when you choose to watch one for an in-game reward. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where it is required, the game shows a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform before ads load. Depending on your choice, Google shows personalised or non-personalised ads. To change your choice, clear the game’s app data or reinstall it so the form appears again, or email us.

How the Game Checks the Device Clock

To prevent cheating by changing the device clock, the game, when online, sends one secure request to https://www.google.com/generate_204 to read the current time. Progress made while the device clock was changed may be undone. Nothing is sent beyond what any web request carries; Google receives your IP address as part of the request.

In-App Purchases

Purchases (coins, booster packs and a one-time starter pack) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your card or payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase went through.

Sharing

Information is shared with Google as our advertising provider and as the processor for Firebase. We may disclose information if the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

International Transfers

Google processes data in the United States and other countries, under its own safeguards.

Retention

  • Game data on your device: until you delete the app or its data.
  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.
  • Support emails: until your request is resolved, and at most 2 years.

Your Rights

  • EEA and UK (GDPR): you can ask to access or delete your data, object to processing, withdraw consent, and complain to your data protection authority.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what we collect and to request deletion. We do not sell personal information. Advertising data is shared with Google as described above.
  • To use these rights, email atharmushtaq9@gmail.com. See also Data Deletion.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If we learn that we collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete it.

Security

Data sent by the Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.
