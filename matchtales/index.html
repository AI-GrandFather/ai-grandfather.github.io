Match Tales: Puzzle Adventure

Swap, match and build colorful puzzle combos.

Match Tales is a match 3 puzzle game for Android and iOS by Mian Muhammad Athar, publishing as AI Grandfather. Line up three or more matching pieces and work toward each level's goals within the available moves.

  • Combine special pieces and use available boosters to tackle puzzle goals.
  • Core puzzles run locally. Ads, purchases, purchase restoration and remote settings need an internet connection.
  • The inspected implementation has local progress, optional rewarded ads and optional in-app purchases. It has no publisher account or cloud-save service.

Frequently asked questions

Is Match Tales free to play?

The planned model is free to play with optional purchases and rewarded advertising. Check the official store listing for the final price and available purchases before downloading.

Can I play offline?

Bundled core gameplay is designed to run on your device. Online services, including ads, purchases, restoration and updated game settings, need connectivity. Complete offline behavior of every release feature has not been certified.

What advertising does it use?

The reviewed game code uses optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. No banner or interstitial placements were found in that integration. Ad availability and privacy choices depend on the region, settings and release build.

Can I sync progress between devices?

The inspected implementation saves progress locally and has no publisher cloud-sync service. Device backups may behave differently. Contact Support before clearing data or reinstalling.

What levels, obstacles and power-ups are included?

The game contains matching challenges, special pieces and boosters. The final launch content and player-facing names are being confirmed, so this page does not promise a level count or a specific obstacle roster.
