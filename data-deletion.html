1. Send a privacy request

Email atharmushtaq9@gmail.com with the subject "Block Crush privacy request". Tell us whether you want access, correction or deletion and which information or feature your request concerns. Mian Muhammad Athar (AI Grandfather), Pakistan, handles requests concerning information under our control.

2. Verify the relevant records

We may ask you to reply from the email address used for a support request, provide a relevant purchase reference, or supply an appropriate account or app-instance identifier. Only provide information reasonably necessary to locate and verify the record. An installation date or device model alone may be insufficient to locate Analytics information. Never send passwords, full payment-card details or store credentials.

3. Local game data and backups

Game progress, settings, virtual balances, rewards and purchase-delivery markers are saved locally. Removing the app or clearing its data can permanently remove progress from the device. iOS offloading may preserve app data; deleting an app, clearing storage and offloading are different actions. OS or device backups may retain copies independently.

Deleting local data does not cancel subscriptions or automatically remove server records. Do not reinstall solely to change ad consent: it can lose progress and does not guarantee a new privacy prompt.

4. Optional recovery and authentication

If your build has cloud recovery enabled, identify the linked recovery account or relevant identifier in your request. We will assess deletion of the recovery profile and authentication records we control, subject to necessary verification and retention requirements. Cloud data and local saves are separate; deleting one does not guarantee deletion of the other. Removing Apple or Google sign-in access through a provider is also separate from deleting records held by us.

5. Purchases, subscriptions and fraud-prevention records

Purchase verification and delivery records may include product and transaction identifiers, entitlement state and hashed purchase-token identities. Some records may be retained to prevent replay or duplicate grants, handle refunds or disputes, or meet legal obligations. We will explain applicable limits rather than promise deletion of all purchase records.

Apple and Google independently keep store and billing records. Manage subscription cancellation, purchase history and refund requests through the store used to purchase. A privacy request does not cancel a subscription or create a refund.

6. Analytics, crashes and advertising

We can act on records we control and use available Firebase or provider deletion tools where relevant records can be located and a request verified. Advertising partners and other platforms may independently process information under their own policies. We cannot promise to delete every provider record.

Use the in-game consent prompts and available privacy controls, Ad Privacy Choices when shown, and OS tracking or advertising-ID controls for future processing. These controls do not automatically delete past data. See the Privacy Policy for provider links.

7. Gaming profiles and support records

Manage Game Center or Google Play Games profile information through the respective platform. Local game records and platform leaderboard records are separate. We can assess deletion of support emails and related records under our control, subject to legal, security and dispute-related exceptions.

8. Retention, response and your rights

We may retain limited information where necessary and lawful for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, transaction reconciliation or disputes. We will explain limitations affecting your request and respond within deadlines required by applicable law. We do not promise an unsupported fixed deletion period for every provider or record.

Depending on applicable law, you may also have access, correction, portability, objection, restriction, consent-withdrawal or appeal rights. Authorized-agent requests may require verification. Parents and guardians may contact us about a child's information. See the Privacy Policy for regional rights and contact details.
