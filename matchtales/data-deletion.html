Data Deletion

Last updated: November 9, 2026

You can request deletion even though the inspected game has no publisher account. This page covers local game data, support correspondence and information handled through service providers. Contact Mian Muhammad Athar (AI Grandfather), Pakistan: atharmushtaq9@gmail.com.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email atharmushtaq9@gmail.com with the subject "Match Tales Data Deletion".
  2. Describe which information you want deleted: for example support correspondence or identifiable Analytics records. Include the platform and any relevant support-thread or transaction reference. Device model and approximate installation date can provide context but may not uniquely identify a record.
  3. We may ask for limited additional information to locate records and verify your authority, such as a reply from the original email address. An app-instance identifier may be necessary for provider-linked data; we will explain if we cannot retrieve or match it safely.

Do not send passwords, payment-card information, store login codes or a complete device backup. We will assess data within our control, use available provider deletion tools and explain any inability to identify a record, lawful retention exception or further provider request needed. We respond within applicable legal deadlines; no unverified 7-day or 30-day completion guarantee is made.

Remove data stored on your device

Deleting local data may permanently remove levels, balances, boosters, settings, event state and local transaction-delivery records. Contact Support first about unresolved purchases. Local deletion is separate from refund eligibility and provider-side deletion.

  • Android: Settings > Apps > Match Tales > Storage (or Storage & cache) > Clear storage/data. Labels vary by device. Uninstalling also removes ordinary app-local storage, subject to backup and restore behavior.
  • iOS: Settings > General > iPhone Storage (or iPad Storage) > Match Tales > Delete App. Offload App preserves app documents and data and is not the same action.
  • Backups: Review Google or iCloud/device backup settings separately. Restoring a backup may restore old data. Deleting an installed app does not necessarily remove backup copies.

Provider records and advertising identifiers

Uninstalling, deleting local storage, changing an advertising ID or withdrawing tracking permission does not erase records already held by Google, Apple, Unity or other providers. Stores can retain purchase and financial records. Advertising, Analytics and hosting data remain subject to provider settings, lawful retention and available rights.

For data processed on our behalf, contact us so we can assess the available controls. For information a provider controls independently, use its privacy-request process, including Google privacy controls, Firebase information, Apple privacy and Unity privacy. We cannot guarantee erasure of independent provider records.

What may remain

Relevant records may be kept where necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, transaction reconciliation or resolving claims, subject to applicable law. We will explain an applicable exception when responding. Information that is truly no longer identifiable may remain in statistics; replacing a name with an identifier does not by itself make data anonymous.

Consent and parental requests

Deletion is separate from future consent choices. See the Privacy Policy for device controls and current limitations. Parents or guardians can contact us about a child's information and may be asked to verify their authority using proportionate information. See Support for lost purchases or progress.
