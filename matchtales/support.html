Player Support

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Contact Mian Muhammad Athar, publishing as AI Grandfather, at atharmushtaq9@gmail.com. Include "Match Tales Support" and a short description in your subject. Replies depend on the request and available records; no fixed turnaround is promised.

Keep your progress safe. Contact us before uninstalling or clearing app storage. These actions may erase local progress and consumables. Never send passwords, store login codes or payment-card details.

Missing or pending purchases

  1. Check the original Apple or Google account's purchase history and whether the payment completed or remains pending.
  2. Reopen the game with a stable connection and the original store account. Allow the store to finish its purchase check. Do not buy the item again just to trigger delivery.
  3. For eligible one-time items, use Restore Purchases in the home-screen settings. It does not restore spent coins, all consumables or level progress. Availability can vary in the final build.
  4. If still missing, email the platform, app version, product name, approximate date and order/transaction reference. Send a redacted receipt only if needed; hide unrelated purchases and personal or payment details.

The reviewed code attempts to recover unfinished purchases, but delivery and restoration need final store testing. Purchases do not automatically transfer between Apple and Google accounts or platforms.

Refunds

Use the store where you purchased: Apple refund requests or Google Play refunds. Store policies and applicable consumer law determine eligibility. Contact us for undelivered or defective items; a store referral does not remove your rights against the publisher.

Lost progress or a new device

The inspected game stores progress locally and has no publisher account or cloud-sync service. Restoration from operating-system backups may vary. Tell us your device model, OS and app version, last known level, approximate time and what changed. We can investigate available evidence but cannot promise save recovery. Restoring purchases is separate from restoring gameplay.

Game will not load, crashes or runs slowly

Close and reopen the game, check for updates, restart the device and check available storage. Use automatic device date and time. If an ad, purchase or remote service fails, check connectivity. Avoid deleting app data as an initial troubleshooting step. Send reproduction steps, the affected level and a screenshot if useful; remove private notifications from screenshots.

Ads and missing rewards

The reviewed implementation uses optional rewarded AdMob ads. Ads may be unavailable because of inventory, connection or privacy settings. For a missing reward, send the approximate time, placement and what happened; do not repeatedly watch ads to diagnose a failure.

Privacy choices and reminders

Review the Privacy Policy for tracking and advertising choices. Device settings can manage tracking permissions and notifications. A working in-game control to reopen ad privacy choices was not verified. Email us for assistance; do not reinstall or clear data solely to reset consent, and do not assume an email switches off SDK processing.

Privacy or deletion requests

See Data Deletion. We may need proportionate information to locate and verify the relevant records. We respond to statutory requests within applicable legal deadlines, without promising a universal number of days.
