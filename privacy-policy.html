This policy covers the Block Crush mobile game on supported platforms, its pages on ai-grandfather.github.io and player support. It does not cover unrelated studio games. Features and service availability can differ by platform, app version, region and configuration.

2. Information you provide

Support emails contain your email address, message and any attachments you send. You may provide an app version, device model, purchase reference or screenshot to help resolve a problem. Please do not send passwords, store credentials, full payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

A chosen player name, avatar, theme, scores, progress and preferences are part of your game profile. Most gameplay state is saved on your device. Platform gaming or optional recovery features can process additional profile information as described below.

3. Device, gameplay and network information

Depending on enabled services and your choices, the game and its providers process app or installation identifiers, device and operating-system information, app version, language, session and gameplay activity, diagnostics and network information such as IP addresses. Advertising services may infer approximate location from IP addresses. The game does not request precise GPS location for gameplay.

Gameplay measurement can include modes played, level starts and outcomes, scores, challenges, rewards, offers, customization, ad interactions and purchase-delivery events. Identifiers can connect activity over time. We do not describe information as anonymous merely because it does not contain your name.

4. Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics

Google Analytics for Firebase measures app use and gameplay interactions. It can process app-instance identifiers, device and regional information and gameplay events. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, stack traces, device and app context and diagnostic information to investigate reliability problems.

When Analytics and crash reporting are available, each has a separate stored choice. Their collection is enabled only when the corresponding service is enabled and your choice is granted. Available prompts or privacy settings let you make those choices. Changing a choice affects future collection; it does not automatically erase previously received information.

These controls concern Analytics and Crashlytics, rather than every Firebase service. Purchase verification, authentication, configuration delivery and enabled recovery have separate purposes. See Firebase privacy and security information.

5. Remote configuration

Firebase Remote Config retrieves app-wide feature configuration, including settings controlling optional spawning models. Google can process Firebase installation identifiers and associated app, device and connection information to deliver configuration. The app's configuration request does not upload your board or saved progress. Cached settings and bundled defaults can be used when the service is unavailable. Remote Config is separate from Analytics consent and is not a cloud backup.

6. Advertising and mediation partners

Block Crush includes Google AdMob for banners, interstitials and optional rewarded advertising. Advertising services can process IP addresses, advertising and other app/device identifiers, ad impressions, clicks, reward-completion signals, device information and diagnostics for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

The game includes mediation integrations for AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource and Liftoff Monetize. Which provider serves a particular ad depends on the active AdMob configuration, availability, region and privacy choices. An included integration does not mean every provider receives every player's data on every session. The consent message, where shown, identifies applicable advertising providers.

Provider information: Google, AppLovin, Unity Ads and ironSource, and Liftoff. Also see Google partner-services information.

7. Ad consent, tracking permission and choices

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates advertising-consent information and presents messages where required by the configured region and service. The game checks advertising eligibility before requesting ads. When required, an Ad Privacy Choices control in Settings lets you reopen the privacy-options form.

On iOS, App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission governs tracking under Apple's rules and access to the advertising identifier. You can review permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Android advertising-ID and ad-privacy controls are available through device settings, depending on OS version.

UMP choices, ATT permission and Analytics/crash-reporting choices serve different purposes. Declining ATT does not necessarily stop all advertising or all other data processing. Non-personalized ads can still use device storage, identifiers and connection information and may still require consent. Available ad types and choices depend on applicable law and provider configuration.

8. Purchases, subscriptions and verification

Apple or Google processes store payments. We do not receive your full payment-card details from the stores. The game handles product identifiers, store transaction identifiers, purchase receipts or signed transaction data, entitlement status, subscription expiry and delivery records to verify and deliver purchases and restore eligible entitlements.

The purchase-verification service uses Google Cloud/Firebase hosting and can receive purchase proof, product and platform information, authentication tokens and app-attestation signals. Anonymous Firebase authentication and Firebase App Check support purchase security where enabled. Server records include transaction and delivery state and hashed purchase-token identities for duplicate prevention and reconciliation. Store notifications can update renewal, expiry, refund or revocation status.

These security and transaction records are separate from optional usage Analytics. Cancelling a subscription stops future renewal according to the store's rules; deleting the app or requesting data deletion does not itself cancel a subscription or create a refund. Manage subscriptions and refund requests through the store used to purchase. See Apple privacy information and Google privacy information.

9. Local saves and optional cloud recovery

The game stores progress, scores, virtual balances, inventory, customization, settings, reward completion and purchase-delivery markers on your device. Device or OS backups may preserve copies independently. Removing the app or clearing its storage can remove local progress; restoration depends on your device and any enabled recovery service.

Where optional cloud recovery is available, it uses Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. When enabled, an anonymous account can identify a recovery profile; linking Apple or Google sign-in can associate it with that provider identity. Recovery synchronizes the supported profile, progress and transaction-related markers. Provider sign-in may supply account identifiers and permitted account attributes. This feature is not available in every app version and is not a guarantee of cross-device recovery. Block Crush does not offer Facebook Login.

10. Game Center and Google Play Games

Available online leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games Services. These platforms can process your gaming identity, display name, scores and achievement activity. Information submitted to a leaderboard may be visible to others according to the platform's settings. Manage your gaming profile and visibility through the applicable platform. Local in-game records are separate from online leaderboard records.

11. Reminders and notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled locally, subject to your settings and OS permission. The app stores scheduling preferences and reminder state on the device. This reminder system does not require a remote push-notification token. You can turn reminders off in the game and manage notification permission in device settings.
