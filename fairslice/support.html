FAQ

How do lives work?

You have up to 10 lives, and one refills every 30 minutes. When you run out, you can watch an optional ad or spend coins for another life, or wait for a refill.

What is the Slice Pass?

An optional 28-day pass that raises your maximum lives to 15 and includes Live Percentages. It does not renew automatically, so you will never be charged again unless you buy it again.

How do I restore purchases?

Open the Store in the app and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings > Reset progress. This deletes stars, coins, knives, achievements, stats and your daily streak on this device. Settings are kept. This cannot be undone.

How do I change my ad and privacy choices?

Use the privacy settings in the app, and your device’s tracking or ad ID settings.
