Privacy Policy

Updated November 26, 2026

Merge Jar is published by Mian Muhammad Athar, an independent developer in Pakistan publishing as AI Grandfather. This policy covers the Merge Jar game, this website and player support. Contact atharmushtaq9@gmail.com.

Game progress

The game saves scores, current jars, settings, coins, boosters and daily rewards on your device. The current game does not require an account and has no chat or social login. We do not operate a cloud save service for Merge Jar. Your device or platform backup settings may include local application data.

Advertising and consent

Merge Jar uses Google Mobile Ads and Google User Messaging Platform for ads and consent. The game includes Google mediation adapters for Unity Ads and Liftoff Monetize. Depending on the build, region, consent and device settings, the services involved in an ad request may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostic information. These services may use that information to serve and measure ads, protect against fraud and diagnose errors.

Your choices

Use Privacy Choices in the game's settings where the consent form is available. On iOS you can manage tracking permission in Settings under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Device settings and ad-provider controls may offer additional choices. Read Data deletion for local saves and support requests.

Support, hosting and retention

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you send. We use it to respond and handle your request, and keep it as needed for that purpose and applicable obligations. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and has no application analytics scripts or submission forms. See GitHub’s privacy statement for information about hosting.

Children and privacy requests

Merge Jar has no feature for creating a child account or submitting a player profile. For concerns about a child's information, or to ask about access or deletion, contact atharmushtaq9@gmail.com. Advertising providers handle their own records under their respective policies.
