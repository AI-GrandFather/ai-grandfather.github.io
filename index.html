Frequently Asked Questions

What is Block Crush: Fun Puzzle Game?
It is an independent minimalist puzzle application designed for iOS platforms. The gameplay core focuses on strategic line clearances and geometric block layout matching to stimulate logical focus.
How much does it cost to play?
The game is 100 percent free to download and install via the Apple App Store. There are zero mandatory paywalls, locked progression ranks, or hidden micropayments required to complete challenges.
Do you need an active internet connection to complete levels?
No. The entire puzzle calculation engine runs locally inside your mobile operating ecosystem. It remains 100 percent functional offline without any cellular data connection or active wireless network link.
What makes this game different from standard puzzle apps?
This application deliberately omits loud arcade loops, flashing banners, and modern attention-grabbing menus. It utilizes a dark ambient color scheme crafted to assist deep focus and reduce cognitive stress.
How long does a typical puzzle session last?
Individual rounds possess no forced operational countdown timers and last until you run out of empty grid placements. Game sessions scale dynamically, lasting from a quick two-minute logic break to a twenty-minute strategy layout run.
Do you support all regions on the App Store?
Yes, the product is published worldwide across all distribution regions supported by Apple App Store Connect. Its core layout relies completely on universal geometric patterns, requiring no local translation barriers.
