1. Who we are and what this covers

Mian Muhammad Athar, an independent developer based in Pakistan and publishing as AI Grandfather, is responsible for information processed for Block Crush: Classic Puzzle where we determine its purposes and means. Contact atharmushtaq9@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Block Crush mobile game, its pages on ai-grandfather.github.io and player support. Features and service availability can differ by platform, version, region and configuration.

2. Information you provide

If you contact support, we receive your email address, message and any information or attachments you choose to send. A player name, avatar, theme, scores and preferences you enter are used as part of your game profile; most profile and gameplay state is kept locally. Please do not send passwords, store credentials, full payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

3. Device, gameplay and network information

When enabled services operate, the app and its providers may process app or installation identifiers, device and operating-system details, app version, language, session and gameplay events, diagnostics, and network information such as IP addresses. Ad services may infer approximate location from network information. The game does not request precise GPS location for gameplay.

Gameplay events can describe game modes, level starts and outcomes, scores, challenges, rewards, customization, ad activity and purchase delivery. These events are designed to avoid direct identifiers and free-form text, but that does not mean all provider-held data is anonymous.

4. Analytics and crash reports

Google Analytics for Firebase measures app use and allowlisted gameplay events. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports and app/device context to help diagnose failures. When Firebase collection is configured, unknown Analytics and crash-reporting preferences are set to enabled by default. Existing explicit opt-outs are retained. Collection is also subject to the app's service configuration.

Analytics and crash reporting have separate controls in the game's Settings privacy section. These controls do not govern advertising, purchase verification, configuration delivery or optional recovery. Changing a choice affects future collection and does not itself erase information already received by a provider. See Firebase privacy and security information.

5. Remote configuration

Firebase Remote Config can deliver app-wide feature configuration. Google may process Firebase installation, app, device and connection information for this service. The app's configuration request does not send your board or saved progress. Remote Config operates separately from Analytics collection and is not a cloud backup.

6. Advertising and advertising choices

Block Crush includes Google AdMob for ads, including banner, interstitial and optional rewarded formats. The app also includes mediation adapters for AppLovin, Unity Ads, ironSource and Liftoff Monetize. Which provider participates depends on the active ad configuration, region, availability and applicable privacy choices; an included adapter does not mean every provider receives data in every session.

Advertising providers may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, reward-completion signals, device information and diagnostics for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates consent information and may display a message or privacy-options form where required by region and service. iOS App Tracking Transparency (ATT) and device advertising settings are separate from UMP and Analytics choices. Ads and related data processing can therefore differ by platform, region, app version, consent and provider availability. Use the privacy choices presented in the app and your device settings where available.

Provider information: Google, AppLovin, Unity, Liftoff, and Google partner services.

7. Purchases and purchase verification

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments. The game handles product identifiers, transaction or purchase proof, entitlement and subscription state, and delivery records to verify purchases, prevent duplicate grants, deliver items and restore eligible entitlements. We do not receive your full payment-card details from the stores.

Where purchase verification is available, it uses Firebase Authentication, Firebase App Check and a Google Cloud/Firebase service. To verify a purchase, the service receives a platform purchase token or signed transaction, product and platform information, authentication and app-attestation signals. Its transaction records include purchase and delivery status, subscription lifecycle information, and a hashed token reference used to help prevent duplicate grants. We keep these records as needed to process transactions, prevent duplicate delivery, address refunds or revocations, and meet legal obligations. Apple, Google and infrastructure providers may also retain transaction records under their own policies.

Purchase verification is separate from optional Analytics. Deleting the app or asking us to delete data does not cancel a store subscription or itself qualify you for a refund. Manage billing and refund requests through the store used for purchase. See Apple privacy information and Google privacy information.

8. Local saves and optional recovery

Progress, scores, virtual balances, inventory, profile customization, settings, reward state and purchase-delivery markers are saved on your device. Device backups may keep separate copies. Removing the app or clearing its storage can remove local state.

Where optional cloud recovery is available and enabled, Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore may process an anonymous Firebase identity, profile and supported game state. Linking Apple or Google sign-in can associate recovery with that provider account. Availability depends on build and configuration; cross-device recovery is not guaranteed. Block Crush does not offer Facebook Login.

9. Game Center and Google Play Games

When online achievements or leaderboards are available and used, Apple Game Center or Google Play Games Services may process your platform gaming identity, display name, scores and achievement activity. Leaderboard details can be visible according to the platform's rules and your account settings. Manage those records and visibility through the relevant platform.

10. Local reminders

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device, subject to your in-game setting and operating-system notification permission. The reminder feature uses local scheduling and does not require a remote push-notification token. You can turn it off in the game or manage notification permission in device settings.

11. Sharing and service providers

Information is shared with providers needed for the services described above, including Google/Firebase/Google Cloud, Apple, configured advertising and mediation providers, platform gaming services, our email provider for support, and GitHub for this website. Their privacy terms describe processing they perform for their own purposes. We may also disclose information where required by law or where lawful to protect users, services or legal rights.

Depending on local law, advertising disclosures may be treated as sale, sharing or targeted advertising even when no money changes hands. Use privacy choices made available by the app, platform or relevant provider, and contact us about applicable rights.

12. Website, international processing and retention

This page contains no analytics or advertising scripts, third-party fonts, cookies or browser-storage code. GitHub Pages receives connection information such as your IP address and requested URL when you load it. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The publisher is based in Pakistan. Providers may process information in other countries, including the United States. Information is retained as needed for the relevant service, support, security, legal obligations and disputes. We do not promise one retention period across local data, stores, advertising services and infrastructure; providers may retain their records under their own policies.

Deleting the app can remove local game data, but does not automatically delete provider, store, platform or device-backup records. For information we control, or to ask about access, correction or deletion, email atharmushtaq9@gmail.com with “Block Crush” in the subject. We may request proportionate details to locate and verify a record. Do not send passwords or full payment details. See Data Deletion Instructions.

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct, delete, receive, restrict or object to use of personal information, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising disclosures. Conditions and exceptions apply. We respond within applicable legal deadlines. A request to us does not change device or store settings and cannot remove records controlled independently by a platform or provider.

We use available platform and provider safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We do not guarantee that every local save is encrypted or that data loss or unauthorized access cannot occur.
