Before Contacting Support

  • Confirm that camera access is enabled for FurrFind in iOS Settings.
  • Use a clear, well-lit pet photo with the face and body visible where possible.
  • Restart the app if scanning appears delayed or the camera preview does not load.
  • Include your device model, iOS version, app version, and a short description of the issue when emailing support.

Privacy and Data

FurrFind processes breed scans locally on your device and does not store scan history on external servers. See the Privacy Policy and Data Deletion pages for details.
