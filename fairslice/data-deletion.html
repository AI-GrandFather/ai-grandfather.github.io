Data Deletion

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Fair Slice has no accounts and the developer does not store your game data on any server.

Delete Data on Your Device

  • Reset progress: Settings > Reset progress deletes stars, coins, knives, achievements, stats and your daily streak.
  • Delete everything: uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data, including settings.

Advertising and Analytics Data (Google)

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”).
  • Withdraw consent in the app’s privacy settings to stop personalised ads and analytics.
  • Learn more: Google partner sites policy and Firebase privacy.

Purchases

Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google under their policies.
