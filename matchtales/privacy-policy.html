1. Who we are

Match Tales: Puzzle Adventure ("Match Tales", "the game") is published by Mian Muhammad Athar, an independent developer in Pakistan publishing as AI Grandfather ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal information processed for the game where we decide how and why it is used. Contact: atharmushtaq9@gmail.com.

This policy covers Match Tales version 1.0.0 for iPhone, this website and player support.

2. Summary

  • Match Tales is a free puzzle game. Version 1.0.0 has no in-app purchases, no accounts, no chat and no social login.
  • Your progress is saved on your device. We do not run our own game servers or cloud saves.
  • The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Remote Config and Google AdMob rewarded ads. These Google services process device identifiers, gameplay events, diagnostics and approximate (IP-based) location.
  • We do not collect your name, contacts, photos, precise location, health or payment information.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices in the game's Settings ("Privacy Options", where required) and your tracking choice in iOS Settings.

3. What happens when you first open the game

On first launch, the game asks you to accept the Terms of Use and confirm that you have read this Privacy Policy. None of the services described below that collect data start before you do this.

After that, in this order:

  1. Ad privacy choices. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) shows a consent form for advertising and related data use.
  2. App Tracking Transparency. If ads may be requested, iOS asks whether you allow the game to track you. You can say no and still play and watch rewarded ads.
  3. Analytics and game settings. Google Analytics for Firebase starts, with Google consent mode set from your answer to the consent form. Firebase Remote Config downloads game tuning values.
  4. Rewarded ads. Google AdMob is initialised so you can choose to watch a rewarded ad.

The game does not ask for notification permission at this point. See section 10.

4. Information we collect

Device identifiers. The identifier for vendor (IDFV), the Firebase app-instance ID and Firebase installation ID. The Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) is only accessed if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Approximate location. Google may derive your country or region from your IP address. The game does not request precise (GPS) location.

Gameplay and product interaction. Events such as level start and end, moves used, results, boosters used, rewards earned and spent in the game, screens viewed, settings changes and your answers to the tracking and notification prompts.

Diagnostics. Crash and performance information, device model, operating system, app version, language, and device memory and graphics capability.

Advertising data. Information about rewarded ads you request, view and complete, collected by Google AdMob and recorded in our analytics.

Support emails. If you email us, we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

We do not set a user ID or attach your name or email address to analytics events.

5. Information we do not collect

Match Tales does not collect your name, email address (unless you email support), phone number, contacts, photos or other files, precise location, health or fitness data, or payment or financial information. There are no accounts, no chat, no user-generated content and no social login. Version 1.0.0 has no in-app purchases.

6. Services we use

Google Analytics for Firebase measures how the game is played so we can fix problems and improve levels and balance. It respects the Google consent mode signals set from your consent form answer. If we promote the game with Google Ads campaigns, Google's on-device conversion measurement (part of Firebase Analytics) may be used to measure installs.

Firebase Remote Config delivers game tuning values, such as difficulty and reward settings. It uses the Firebase installation ID and basic device, language and country information. Built-in defaults are used if it cannot connect.

Google AdMob provides optional rewarded ads that give extra moves or lives. There are no banner or interstitial ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rating G). AdMob may use device identifiers, IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. Depending on your choices and location, ads may be personalised or non-personalised. Google may work with other advertising technology providers according to your consent choices; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Time check. When you are online, the game sends a simple request to https://www.google.com/generate_204 to read the current time, so timed rewards cannot be abused by changing the device clock. Google receives normal connection data such as your IP address. No game data is attached.

Read more in the Google Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy information.

7. How we use information

We use information to run the game and deliver game settings, show rewarded ads and give their rewards, measure and improve gameplay, find and fix crashes and performance problems, prevent fraud and abuse, answer support requests, and meet legal obligations.

Where the law of the EEA, UK or Switzerland applies, we rely on your consent for analytics and advertising that require it (given through the consent form), on our legitimate interests for security, fraud prevention and keeping the game working, and on legal obligation where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

8. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers described in this policy: Google (Firebase and AdMob), Apple (App Store and iOS services), our email provider for support, and GitHub, which hosts this website. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect players, the game or others.

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws, using advertising identifiers for personalised ads may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising". You can opt out using the Privacy Options button in Settings (where shown) and by not allowing tracking in iOS.

9. Your choices

  • Privacy Options. Where required, the game's Settings screen has a Privacy Options button that reopens the consent form, so you can change or withdraw your consent at any time.
  • Tracking. You can change your App Tracking Transparency choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Notifications. You can turn reminders off in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Links. Settings also has links to this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Use.
  • Ads are optional. You never have to watch an ad to keep playing.

Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that happened before you withdrew it.

10. Notifications

Match Tales can send local reminders, for example when your lives are full. These are scheduled on your device; we do not use a push notification service. The game asks for notification permission only after you finish level 5. You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings.

11. Progress saved on your device

Your progress, game items and settings are stored on your device. We do not provide accounts or cloud saves, so we cannot restore progress if the app is deleted or the device is lost. iOS device backups may include game data.

12. How long we keep information

Analytics data is kept according to Google's retention settings for our Firebase project, and Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies. Remote Config and installation identifiers are handled by Google as described in its documentation. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you and for our records. Data on your device stays there until you delete the app.

13. Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes the progress and settings stored on your device. To ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device, follow the steps on our Data Deletion page.

14. International processing

We are based in Pakistan. Google, Apple, GitHub and our email provider may process information in other countries, including the United States. Where required, these providers use recognised safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Contact us for more information at atharmushtaq9@gmail.com.

15. Security

We use the security features of iOS and of our service providers to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we limit the data we collect to what the game needs.

16. Children

Match Tales is rated 4+ on the App Store because its content is suitable for all ages, but the game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, contact us at atharmushtaq9@gmail.com and we will help delete it.

17. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. If you live in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, you can also complain to your local data protection authority. If you live in a US state with a privacy law, you may have the right to know, delete and correct personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Because we do not hold accounts, please include the information described on the Data Deletion page so we can find data linked to your device. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

18. This website

The Match Tales pages do not use cookies, analytics or advertising scripts. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log IP addresses and request information; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

19. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the game or the services it uses change. The date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the game and ask for your consent again where required.
