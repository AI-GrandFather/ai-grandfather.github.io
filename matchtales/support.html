FAQ

My purchase didn’t arrive. Close and reopen the game while online; pending Google Play purchases are delivered automatically. If it still hasn’t arrived, email us your order number (it starts with GPA.).

I lost my progress. Progress is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data removes it, and we cannot recover it.

How do I change my ad consent? Clear the game’s app data or reinstall it, and the consent form appears again on the next launch (where it is required). You can also email us.

The game won’t load or is slow. Update to the latest version, restart your device and free up some storage.

How do I delete my data? See Data Deletion.

How do I report a bug? Email us your device model, operating system version, the level number and a screenshot if you can.
