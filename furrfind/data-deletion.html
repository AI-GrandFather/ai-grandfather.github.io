Data Deletion Instructions

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

FurrFind stores scan history locally on your device and does not store data on external servers.

iOS Instructions

To erase your entire local app history, simply delete the app from your device:

  1. Open your iOS device Settings menu.
  2. Navigate to General, then tap iPhone Storage.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap on FurrFind.
  4. Tap Delete App. This safely clears all application configurations, scanning data records, and local history cache footprints immediately.

Android Instructions

If you use FurrFind on Android, open Settings, then Apps, then FurrFind, then tap Uninstall.

Since FurrFind stores no data on external servers, deleting the app permanently removes all locally stored scan history. No further action is required and no request needs to be submitted to the developer.

If you believe any data was collected in error, contact atharmushtaq9@gamil.com and we will respond within 7 days.
