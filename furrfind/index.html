Frequently Asked Questions

What is FurrFind?
It is an independent pet breed identifier application developed for iOS devices. The core utility leverages artificial intelligence to analyze camera frames and photos to detect domestic animal lineages.
How does the breed scanner calculate accuracy metrics?
The calculation matrix compares structural markers from your pet photograph against a machine learning model trained on over 300 distinctive animal classes, outputting a breakdown of percentage confidence matches.
Can I identify mixed breed pets using the tool?
Yes, the pattern engine can process multiple ancestral markers within a single photo submission. The result screen displays a segmented list showing each detected breed trace alongside its distinct characteristic traits.
Do you collect or share my scanned pet photos?
No, your data privacy is fully preserved. All image calculations are processed through highly secure execution layers, and your past scan records stay isolated within the local storage of your mobile operating system.
What animal categories are currently supported?
The utility is engineered explicitly to process domestic dog and cat breeds. It recognizes all officially registered global breed profiles, including complex cross-breeds and indoor kitten variations.
