Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

At a glance

Fair Slice saves game progress and preferences on your device and does not require an account. The app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, Google AdMob, local notifications and Apple StoreKit purchases on supported Apple platforms. The app's services can process device, app, ad, diagnostic and transaction information as described below.

1. Who we are and scope

Fair Slice: Slice & Cut Puzzle is developed by Mian Muhammad Athar, publishing as AI Grandfather. For privacy questions, email atharmushtaq9@gmail.com. This policy covers the app and Fair Slice pages on ai-grandfather.github.io. Features and purchases may differ by platform, app version, region and store availability.

2. Information on your device

The app stores game progress, stars and best results, unlocked cutters and designs, coins, the Perfect-Cut Album, preferences and purchase-entitlement state on your device. The app does not require account registration, login or a password to play. The current code does not provide account-based cloud-save functionality.

If you send a support email, we receive the address, message and any details or attachments you choose to include. Please do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unrelated sensitive information.

3. Analytics and crash reports

The app includes Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics can process app-use and gameplay events; Crashlytics can process crash reports and app/device context to help diagnose reliability issues. The app stores a preference for sharing analytics, and new installations default to enabled. An existing saved preference is respected.

The current Settings screen does not expose an Analytics/Crashlytics switch in the source reviewed for this policy. To ask about changing this preference or the handling of related information, email us. Turning off collection through an available control affects future collection and may not remove information already received by Google. See Firebase privacy and security information.

4. Advertising and ad privacy choices

Fair Slice uses Google AdMob for advertising, including a home banner, ad breaks and optional rewarded ads where enabled. Google and its advertising partners may process device or advertising identifiers, IP address, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, and diagnostic signals to deliver, measure and protect advertising. Approximate location may be inferred from IP address.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) updates advertising-consent information and can show a consent form or privacy-options form where required by configured region and service. When the app shows an Ad Privacy Options control in Settings, it opens the available UMP privacy-options form. Advertising choices are separate from the Analytics/Crashlytics preference and from device-level tracking or advertising controls. Ad personalization and delivery behavior may vary by platform, region, consent state and provider configuration.

See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Purchases

Fair Slice's current source implements Apple StoreKit 2 purchases on supported Apple platforms. Available store products include one-time, non-consumable cosmetic designs and a non-renewing Slice Pass that lasts 28 days. The Pass increases maximum lives from 10 to 15 and includes Live Percentages while active. Coins are earned through play; the current source does not sell coins.

Apple processes payment and store account information. The app receives product and transaction or entitlement information needed to verify, restore and provide purchased features. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details. Prices and availability are shown by Apple at purchase time. The current Android source reviewed for this policy does not include an equivalent purchase implementation. Manage billing and refund requests through the store and under applicable law. Deleting the app does not itself request a refund.

6. Daily reminders

If you choose to enable the daily reminder, the app requests operating-system notification permission and schedules a local notification on your device. Reminders are off by default. The reminder feature does not use a server or remote push token. You can turn it off in the app settings or manage notification permission in device settings.

7. Sharing and third parties

When you share a result card or cut, the app opens your device's system share sheet. The app's share flow does not upload that image to a Fair Slice server. The receiving app or service handles anything you choose to share with it.

Fair Slice uses Google Firebase/AdMob for the services described above and Apple StoreKit for purchases. The provider may process information under its own terms and privacy notices. We may disclose information if required by law or where lawful to protect users, the app or legal rights.

8. Website and international processing

This policy page does not include advertising scripts, analytics, third-party fonts or browser-storage code. GitHub Pages receives connection information such as IP address and requested URL when you load the page. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

The developer is based in Pakistan. Google, Apple, GitHub and email providers may process information in other countries under their terms and applicable safeguards.

9. Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until it is reset, removed with the app or otherwise cleared by the device or operating system. Device backups may retain separate copies. Information handled by Apple, Google or GitHub is subject to those providers' systems and retention settings. We do not promise a fixed retention period for provider-held records.

For help with information we control, email atharmushtaq9@gmail.com. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete provider records or cancel a purchase.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict its use, receive a copy, withdraw consent or opt out of specified advertising uses. Conditions and exceptions apply. Contact us to exercise rights over information we control; provider-held data may also require using the provider's own tools. We respond within applicable legal deadlines.

11. Children and security

Fair Slice is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable law. A parent or guardian with a concern can contact us.

We use available platform and provider safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We do not guarantee that local data cannot be lost or accessed without authorization.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app, services or applicable requirements change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Contact Mian Muhammad Athar, publishing as AI Grandfather, Pakistan, at atharmushtaq9@gmail.com.
