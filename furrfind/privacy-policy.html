Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 20, 2026

FurrFind: AI Breed Identifier is developed by Mian Muhammad Athar. This policy explains how the app handles privacy. FurrFind does not collect, store, sell, rent, or share personal data.

1. Photos and Camera Access

FurrFind uses your device camera or selected photos only to identify dog and cat breeds inside the app. Processing is performed locally on your device. Photos are not uploaded to external servers by FurrFind.

2. Data Collection

FurrFind does not collect names, email addresses, location data, device identifiers, usage analytics, scan photos, or scan results. Any scan history shown in the app is stored locally on your device and can be removed by deleting the app.

3. Third-Party Services

FurrFind does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash reporting tools. If any such service is added in the future, this policy will be updated with 30 days notice before the change takes effect.

4. Data Controller

The data controller is Mian Muhammad Athar. Contact email: atharmushtaq9@gmail.com.

5. Your Rights

Under privacy laws including GDPR, you may have the right to access personal data, request erasure, or object to processing. Because FurrFind does not collect or store personal data, these requests are normally satisfied by the fact that there is no server-side user data to access, erase, or process.

6. Children's Privacy

FurrFind does not knowingly collect personal data from children or adults. Since no personal data is collected, no child profile or account information is created.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect app, legal, or operational changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised Last Updated date.
