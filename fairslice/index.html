Fair Slice: Slice & Cut Puzzle

Every level sets a new target. Cut it exactly.

Fair Slice is a precision cutting puzzle. Read the objective, draw one straight line and watch the pieces split apart with their real percentages: 15/85, 25/75, three shares and more. 1,000 levels across 50 chapters, a Daily Slice, a Weekly Challenge and a Perfect-Cut Album.
