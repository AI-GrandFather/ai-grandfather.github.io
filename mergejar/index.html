Merge Jar:
Drop Puzzle

Drop matching pieces, make bigger merges, and see how much you can fit in the jar.

How to play

How to play

  1. Aim and drop

    Choose where your next piece lands. Pieces roll and settle as the jar fills.

  2. Make a match

    When two identical pieces touch, they combine into a bigger one.

  3. Keep space open

    Plan your next move, use a booster when you need room, and try to beat your best score.

Choose a jar

Build up to a watermelon in Fruit Merge, stack cakes in the bakery, or discover ocean creatures in their own jar. Each theme has its own pieces and best score.
